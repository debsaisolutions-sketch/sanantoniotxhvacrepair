Why Isn't My AC Keeping Up During San Antonio's Extreme Heat?

San Antonio's scorching summer temperatures can push your air conditioning system to its limits, often leaving homeowners wondering why their AC isn't keeping up. Understanding the causes and knowing when to call a professional can help you stay cool, even during the hottest days.

Common Reasons Your AC Struggles in Extreme Heat

1. Insufficient System Size

If your AC unit is too small for your home, it may not be capable of cooling your space efficiently. An undersized system has to work harder, leading to higher energy bills and inadequate cooling. Consider consulting with a local expert to assess if your unit is appropriately sized for your home.

2. Poor Insulation

Without proper insulation, your home can lose cool air rapidly, causing your AC to work overtime. Ensure that your attic and walls are well-insulated to prevent cool air from escaping and hot air from infiltrating.

3. Blocked Air Vents

Furniture or debris blocking air vents can hinder airflow, reducing the efficiency of your cooling system. Make sure vents are open and unobstructed to allow the free flow of air throughout your home.

Maintenance Tips to Boost AC Performance

  • Change Air Filters Regularly: Dirty filters can restrict airflow and reduce efficiency. Replace them every 1-3 months, especially during peak cooling seasons.
  • Clean the Outdoor Unit: Remove debris and foliage around the outdoor condenser unit to improve airflow and efficiency.
  • Schedule Routine Maintenance: Regular inspections by a professional can help identify potential issues before they become costly repairs.

When to Call a Professional

If your AC still struggles after you've tried these DIY solutions, it may be time to contact a local HVAC specialist. Persistent issues could indicate a more serious problem, such as refrigerant leaks or failing components.

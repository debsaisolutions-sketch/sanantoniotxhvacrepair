Why Is My Goodman Furnace Igniter Glowing but Not Igniting a Flame?

If your Goodman furnace's igniter is glowing but you're not getting a flame, it can be both puzzling and frustrating. Understanding the possible causes can help you determine whether it's a DIY fix or if it's time to call a professional in San Antonio.

Common Reasons for Igniter Issues

Faulty Gas Valve

A common reason for an igniter to fail in producing a flame is a malfunctioning gas valve. If the gas valve is stuck or defective, it won't supply gas to the burners, preventing ignition. Checking the gas supply to ensure the valve is open can sometimes resolve this issue.

Blocked Burners

Over time, dust and debris can block the burners. This blockage can prevent the gas from flowing properly, leading to an igniter glow without igniting a flame. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the burners, is crucial for preventing this problem.

Troubleshooting Steps

Check for Error Codes

Many modern furnaces, including Goodman models, have diagnostic lights that flash error codes. Refer to your furnace's manual to interpret these codes, which can offer insight into the problem.

Inspect the Igniter

While the igniter is glowing, it might not be reaching the necessary temperature to light the gas. Inspect the igniter for cracks or wear. If damaged, it will need replacement, a task that might require professional assistance.

  • Turn off power: Before inspecting any parts, ensure the furnace is powered off to avoid injury.
  • Check connections: Secure all electrical connections leading to the igniter.
  • Replacement: If necessary, replace the igniter with a compatible part.

When to Call a San Antonio HVAC Pro

If troubleshooting doesn't resolve the issue, it may be time to call in a professional. Persistent problems like a malfunctioning gas valve or inducer motor that keeps restarting are best handled by experienced HVAC technicians in the San Antonio area.

As a homeowner, it's important to recognize the limits of DIY troubleshooting. Complex issues often require expertise to ensure your furnace operates safely and efficiently. If you're unsure about the next steps, contacting a local HVAC professional can save time and prevent potential hazards.

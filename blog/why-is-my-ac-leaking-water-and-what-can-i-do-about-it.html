Why Is My AC Leaking Water and What Can I Do About It?

It's not uncommon for homeowners in the Texas Hill Country to find their air conditioning units leaking water. However, a significant water leak can indicate a problem that needs urgent attention. In this guide, we'll explore common causes of AC water leaks and when to call a professional in San Antonio for help.

Common Causes of AC Water Leaks

Clogged Condensate Drain Line

The condensate drain line is responsible for expelling moisture collected by your AC. Over time, it can become clogged with dirt, algae, or debris, causing water to back up and leak. Regular cleaning can prevent this issue.

Dirty Air Filters

A dirty air filter can restrict airflow over the evaporator coil, causing it to freeze. When it melts, the excess water may overwhelm the drain pan, leading to leaks. Replacing or cleaning your air filters every 1-3 months can help prevent this.

Damaged or Rusty Drain Pan

If your AC is older, the drain pan may be rusted or damaged, unable to hold water properly. In such cases, replacing the drain pan is necessary to stop leaks.

Low Refrigerant Levels

Low refrigerant can cause the evaporator coil to freeze and eventually thaw, creating excess water that the pan can't manage. This issue often requires a professional to check for leaks and recharge the refrigerant.

When to Call a San Antonio HVAC Professional

  • If cleaning the drain line doesn't stop the leak.
  • When you suspect refrigerant issues or notice ice on the evaporator coil.
  • If the drain pan needs replacement.
  • When regular DIY maintenance doesn't resolve the problem.

While some minor issues can be handled with regular maintenance, significant water leaks often require professional attention. An experienced HVAC technician in San Antonio can quickly diagnose and fix the problem, ensuring your AC runs efficiently and reliably.

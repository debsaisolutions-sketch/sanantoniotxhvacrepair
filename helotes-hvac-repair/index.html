Oak Canopy, Hill Country Pads, and Festival Weekends

Helotes sits northwest along Bandera Road (SH 16) where Bexar County climbs into the Hill Country. Old Town Helotes still hosts Cornyval and market weekends that pack Main Street, while Scenic Loop and Galm Road lead toward Government Canyon State Natural Area—roads our trucks know when reaching acreage homes and ridge-top builds.

Deep live-oak canopy cools yards but packs condenser fins with leaf litter every spring. Newer subdivisions closer to Loop 1604 bake on limestone pads with little shade. The mix creates two failure modes: clogged coils under trees and high-head-pressure shutdowns on bare sun pads.

Bandera Road backups toward the Medical Center / 1604 interchange change afternoon ETAs. Most Helotes emergency calls still land within about 1–2 hours when northwest crews are rolling.
